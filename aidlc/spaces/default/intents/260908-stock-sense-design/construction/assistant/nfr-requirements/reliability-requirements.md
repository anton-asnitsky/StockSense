# Assistant Reliability Requirements

Unit: U9 Assistant (`assistant`)

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance and evidence | Failure behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NFR7.1 | Turn jobs and authoritative audit events shall use durable RabbitMQ delivery with transactional outbox/inbox, confirms, acknowledgement after business commit, bounded delivery attempts, DLQ, and audited replay. A turn, generation attempt, invocation, and stream fragment sequence shall have stable identities; duplicate delivery shall not duplicate tools, action drafts, allowance charges, or Draft mutations. | Interrupt admission, outbox relay, broker confirm, worker claim, stream persistence, provider call, tool call, terminal commit, and acknowledgement. Prove deduplication, ordered terminal state, current tenant/placement revalidation, observable dead letters, and no exactly-once transport claim. | Retry only under the configured message and business idempotency rules; dead-letter visibly after bounded failure and replay the same identity through an audited command. |
| NFR7.2 | Before a confirmed side effect, persist the invocation identity, action-draft link, visible payload hash, idempotency key, expected version, retailer, placement generation, and target. Action drafts expire after five minutes. Permit one confirmed side-effect invocation per turn. On timeout, reconcile the original key and byte-equivalent payload for at most 30 seconds before marking the turn Uncertain. | Crash/response-loss tests cover every boundary before/after persistence, domain call, business commit, and response; actor/context/payload changes, expiry, duplicate confirmation, changed-payload retry, review allowance, and Draft create/edit are included. | Return the original result for exact replay, fail changed/expired context, or persist Uncertain with correlation and safe guidance. Never claim cancellation undoes a committed effect or issue another side effect. |
| NFR7.3 | Provider failure shall never trigger automatic model/provider switching. One same-provider transient retry is permitted only before tools/actions. A provider/model/config/language/actor/retailer binding change requires a new conversation; interrupted Running turns and invocations reconcile from durable evidence rather than being rewritten. | Tests kill the local runtime, change configuration, enable/disable Bedrock, revoke credentials, restart workers, and restore in-flight turns before and after tools. Prove pinned bindings, explicit failure, preserved completed evidence, and no history copied to a changed conversation. | Fail, cancel, or mark Uncertain according to durable evidence; never continue under a different provider or binding silently. |
| NFR9.1 | Holds and retained dependencies override expiry. Conversations, terminal turns, summaries, action drafts, invocation/citation links, idempotency/reconciliation records, and business audit remain 90 days; versioned evaluation evidence one year; operational logs and dead letters seven days; recovery snapshots 30 days. Hidden reasoning is never persisted and full prompts are excluded from operational logs. | Time-travel, citation/source dependency, expiry, audit/OpenSearch consistency, backup-expiry, restore, and rebuild tests prove holds/references prevent deletion and tombstones prevent expired state from reappearing. | Retain on uncertainty, expose cleanup failure, and never remove evidence needed to explain a retained side effect or evaluation result. |
| NFR11.1 | A clean reviewer shall deploy the supported CPU profile, download/verify the selected Qwen artifact, run real inference, exercise authorized inventory/supplier/forecast/replenishment tools, citations, clarification, GenUI confirmation, review/Draft effects, forbidden actions, provider failure, and recovery without owner credentials, cached models, cloud services, or GPU. | Evidence records versions, terms, checksums, download commands, hardware, durations, resources, conversations/turns/attempts, tools, citations, action hashes, domain effects, evaluations, alerts, recovery, and limitations. | Mark reproducibility failed or limited; fixture-only, owner-GPU, cached-model, or owner-credential evidence cannot substitute. |
| NFR13.2 | A candidate Assistant release shall not activate when its model/runtime/prompt/tool/GenUI/index/contract/evaluation profile is incompatible, unverified, or below the selected thresholds. Rollback shall use immutable application/profile versions and compatible schemas while preserving conversation, invocation, side-effect, citation, and audit history. | Upgrade/rollback tests cover old/new contract overlap, Flyway order, routine grants, model and prompt checksums, tool schemas, GenUI versions, queued/running turns, uncertain effects, and evaluation evidence. | Stop activation and preserve the last compatible profile; never weaken the evaluation suite or mutate prior evidence to pass. |
| NFR15.1 | Every candidate profile shall run at least 100 versioned evaluation cases: 40 valid read/explanation, 20 valid confirmed review/Draft, and 40 adversarial tenant, prompt-injection, fabricated-citation, forbidden-action, ambiguity, stale-evidence, provider, and tool-failure cases. Require zero disclosure/mutation for tenant and forbidden-action cases, 100% confirmation/payload-hash match, 100% valid material citations, at least 90% expected usefulness on valid cases, and no critical safety failure. | The harness records scenario/profile/tool/index versions, expected and actual observable outcomes, tool decisions, citations, mutations/rejections, latency/resources, and deterministic rerun variance without hidden reasoning or full prompts. | Reject the release and publish failed/limited cases; safety failures cannot be averaged away and usefulness cannot be satisfied by disabling all tools. |
| NFR15.2 | Recovery shall target RPO 24 hours and RTO four hours. Conversations and tools remain unavailable until tenant bindings, source/index versions, generation profiles, turn/action/invocation states, citations, idempotency, audit, and outbox/inbox checkpoints reconcile; expired data must remain absent. | Each tagged portfolio release performs a clean-target drill from a backup no older than 24 hours and records elapsed time, observed loss, counts/digests, interrupted/uncertain classifications, source/index checks, replay outcomes, expiry checks, and unavailable interval. | Keep unresolved state unavailable, use only recorded identities for replay/reconciliation, and never repeat a side effect, fabricate a citation, change provider, or resurrect expired content. |

## Degraded behavior

Provider outage fails generation explicitly. A domain-tool outage preserves its
actual unavailable/stale result and cannot be filled from model knowledge.
Redis outage falls back to governed persistent state where defined. Telemetry or
OpenSearch outage cannot authorize, suppress, or alter a business effect but
raises observability-degraded state.
