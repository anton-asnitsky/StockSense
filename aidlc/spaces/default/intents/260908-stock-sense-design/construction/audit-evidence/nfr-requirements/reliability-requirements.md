# Audit Evidence Reliability Requirements

Unit: U10 Audit Evidence (`audit-evidence`)

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance and evidence | Failure behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NFR7.1 | C15 delivery shall use durable RabbitMQ, publisher confirms, a unique inbox receipt, immutable derived-event retention, idempotent projection, and a verified checkpoint before acknowledgement. Exact duplicate event ID and digest returns the recorded state; the same ID with different immutable content is quarantined. | Crash-point tests interrupt delivery, authentication, validation, inbox/event commit, index write, verification, checkpoint commit, and acknowledgement. Prove no loss, no duplicate logical projection, stable digest handling, and restart recovery. | Retry the original identity or quarantine/dead-letter visibly; never invent a new ID, overwrite immutable evidence, or claim exactly-once transport. |
| NFR7.2 | Each consumer attempt has a 30-second deadline. Under C15 v2, a message receives five total delivery attempts including the initial delivery, with exponential backoff from one to 30 seconds, then seven-day DLQ retention. One replay per queue processes batches of at most 100 and preserves original message/event identity, canonical payload digest, and producer binding; every replay is audited. Queue/byte caps and capacity failure follow NFR2.5. | Tests exercise transient and terminal failures, all five deliveries, attempt exhaustion, DLQ expiry, replay interruption, exact/changed payload and digest, stale placement, producer/current authorization, 30-second lag, and the 5,000-message/256 MiB queue boundaries. | Dead-letter after the fifth failed delivery, keep safe diagnostics and original identity, reject publishes at the queue cap without silent loss, and refuse replay that fails current authority, expiry, schema, digest, route, or capacity checks. |
| NFR7.3 | Projection writes shall be byte-equivalent and idempotent by event ID. A prior successful index write with a lost checkpoint is verified by digest before checkpoint repair. Operational telemetry loss or OpenSearch failure shall not block producer business commits. | Failure-injection tests cover OpenSearch outage, partial bulk result, timeout after write, checkpoint outage, telemetry outage, restart, duplicate delivery, and digest mismatch. | Preserve retained derived evidence, expose Pending/Failed/Stale/Unavailable state, retry only under the bounded policy, and never authorize or rewrite producer truth. |
| NFR9.1 | Authoritative and derived business audit shall use a 90-day default and operational logs seven days. Producer, U10, and OpenSearch cleanup and reconciliation shall complete within one hour of a versioned cutoff. Policy/run/receipt/count/digest/failure/watermark/tombstone evidence is retained one year and protected recovery snapshots 30 days. Holds override expiry. | Time-travel tests cover holds, cutoff boundaries, producer receipts, partial cleanup, delayed delivery, DLQ, replay, rollback, restore, rebuild, snapshot expiry, and PostgreSQL/OpenSearch reconciliation. | Retain on uncertainty, expose partial/failed cleanup, never advance an unproven watermark, and never resurrect expired records. |
| NFR11.1 | A reviewer shall deploy the CPU-only local stack and execute ingestion, duplicate/mismatch handling, authorized query, stale/unavailable query, DLQ/replay, rebuild/cutover/rollback, retention, tenant movement, and recovery without owner credentials, cached artifacts, cloud services, or GPU. | Evidence records pinned versions/checksums, commands, hardware, resources, event/query identities, counts, digests, checkpoints, routes, alerts, outcomes, and limitations. | Mark reproducibility failed; fixtures, owner credentials, cached images/models, or unmeasured assertions cannot substitute for the real service path. |
| NFR13.2 | Flyway schema/routine/grant changes, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI contracts, event representations, index mappings, and route/checkpoint compatibility shall block rollout when incompatible. Rollback uses immutable application versions and a prior validated generation while applying current expiry watermarks. | Upgrade/rollback tests cover old/new contract overlap, migration order, stored-routine grants, in-flight messages, schema/index version mismatch, route expected versions, and retention changes. | Stop rollout and retain the last compatible application and active generation; never mutate accepted history or select a generation that can resurrect expired evidence. |
| NFR15.1 | Recovery shall use PostgreSQL/configuration backup no older than 24 hours and target RTO two hours. Each retailer route remains unavailable until inbox/event counts, digests, checkpoints, schemas, placement, quarantines/DLQ, replay/retention controls, expiry watermarks, self-audit, and cross-tenant isolation reconcile in a new inactive generation. | Each tagged release performs a clean-target drill and records backup age, elapsed time, loss, counts/digests, mismatches, repairs, exclusions, route activation, and unavailable interval. | Keep mismatched routes unavailable, fence stale work, and never expose mixed, foreign, uncheckpointed, or resurrected evidence. |
| NFR15.2 | One retailer's projection shall move within 15 minutes, with no more than five minutes of rejected work for that retailer and no impact to unrelated retailers. Route activation requires expected old/new placement and route versions plus count/digest/checkpoint/isolation validation. | Exercise drain, active delivery, replay, stale placement, failed target build, retry, atomic cutover, rollback window, and unrelated-retailer query/ingestion traffic. | Preserve the old valid route until cutover, reject stale work, leave failed target generations inactive, and retain revision-bound movement evidence. |

## Maintenance controls

Replay and rebuild require a dry run, bounded source and retailer scope, current
operator authority, expected request/route/policy versions, reason, and
payload-bound idempotency. Cancellation is accepted only before route
activation. The prior validated generation remains available for 24 hours,
subject to current expiry watermarks.
