## Rules summary

| Group | Focus | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BR1 | Human and machine authority | Recovery actions require the right current principal and exact confirmation. |
| BR2 | Roster and deadlines | Each run freezes complete registration values and deadlines, and revalidates live registration before prepare. |
| BR3 | Barrier and delivery | Prepare is recorded before send; only valid durable acknowledgements close the cut. |
| BR4 | Manifest integrity | An internal journal gathers evidence; a complete immutable C26 document is finalized with the terminal outcome and verified before GET. |
| BR5 | Failure and terminal safety | Abort, restart and reconciliation preserve unresolved fences and honest outcomes. |
| BR6 | Visibility and audit | Operator status, durable audit and measured recovery evidence remain traceable. |
