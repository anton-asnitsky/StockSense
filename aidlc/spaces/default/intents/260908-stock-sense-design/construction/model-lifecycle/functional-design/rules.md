Unit: U6 Model Lifecycle (model-lifecycle)

## Rules summary

| Group | Focus | Key outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BR1 | Tenant and Operator authority | Every dataset, artifact, release, route, and action remains retailer-isolated. |
| BR2 | Dataset identity and leakage | Immutable manifests and origin-time evidence prevent hidden data leakage. |
| BR3 | Comparable evaluation | Forecast and inventory metrics use common cohorts and approved arithmetic. |
| BR4 | Training and provenance | The first candidate and every run are deterministic, inspectable, and checksummed. |
| BR5 | Jobs and messaging | One heavy job runs at a time with retained attempts and idempotent recovery. |
| BR6 | Release control | Validation, promotion, and rollback are explicit, audited, and concurrency-safe. |
| BR7 | Forecasting handoff | Forecast Runs pin one verified immutable release without silent fallback. |
| BR8 | Restore and retention | Recovery reconciles every authority before use and preserves expired identity. |
| BR9 | Contracts, audit, and platform | Governed contracts, atomic evidence, explicit prerequisites, and honest proof support delivery. |
