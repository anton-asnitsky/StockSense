# Forecasting Reliability Requirements

Unit: U7 Forecasting (`forecasting`)

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance and evidence | Failure behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NFR7.1 | RabbitMQ shall use confirms, transactional outbox/inbox, acknowledgement after local commit, and tenant-scoped operation keys plus canonical hashes. A 60-second execution lease renews every 15 seconds; four missed heartbeats fence the worker. Each logical run allows three attempts with 30-second-to-five-minute exponential backoff plus jitter; a 15-minute queue timeout consumes a transient attempt under the same run. | Interrupt scheduler, admission, outbox relay, broker, dependency resolution, artifact load, worker, heartbeat, staged output, finalization, pointer commit, and acknowledgement around each boundary. Prove exact replay, changed-payload conflict, fencing, no duplicate daily effect/revision, and immutable prior attempts. | Retry queue expiry, transient dependencies, worker loss, and verified checkpoints only. Data/schema/checksum/tenant/authorization/model/output/resource-limit failures are terminal. Exhaustion finalizes Failed and enters DLQ. |
| NFR9.1 | Dependencies and holds override retention. After they end, published current/superseded requests, runs, series, provenance, idempotency, acknowledgements, and pointer history remain one year; unpublished partial/failed runs and attempts 90 days; staged output seven days; business audit 90 days; logs and dead letters seven days; recovery snapshots 30 days. Replay batches contain at most 20 runs. | Time-travel, downstream-reference, expiry, restore, pointer-history, and replay tests prove planning/purchasing/model/recovery/audit dependencies block deletion and tombstones prevent reappearance. | Retain on uncertainty, keep failed cleanup visible, and never make expired output current or replay-eligible. |
| NFR11.1 | A clean reviewer shall deploy the CPU path, activate a validated baseline or trained release, schedule all three retailers, produce real 28-day series, exercise retry/partial acknowledgement/current selection/freshness/cache, and restore the service without owner credentials, cached artifacts, cloud services, or GPU. | Evidence records versions, model terms/checksums/downloads, commands, hardware, durations, resources, requests/runs/attempts, product outcomes, pointers, alerts, recovery, and limitations. | Mark reproducibility failed; fixtures-only or owner-machine/GPU evidence cannot substitute. |
| NFR13.2 | Flyway/routine incompatibility, contract mismatch, decimal/schema/runtime incompatibility, or unsupported model serialization shall block rollout or forecast activation. | Upgrade/rollback tests use immutable app/model versions, compatible schemas, verified routine grants, migration order, and old/new contract overlap. | Stop rollout/activation and preserve the last compatible deployment and current pointer. |
| NFR15.1 | Over a 30-day seeded schedule test, Forecasting shall create exactly one logical request per retailer-local date, admit at least 99% within five minutes of the resolved 02:00 due instant, and publish a complete current revision by 02:30 when dependencies are healthy. | Exercise 90 retailer-days including clock-forward/back, restarts, duplicate schedulers, broker delay, and healthy/error fixtures. Any duplicate fails; admission and publication percentages, late reasons, and freshness outcomes are published. | Alert at five minutes without admission and 30 minutes without complete publication; preserve the prior pointer and six-hour freshness boundary without substitution. |
| NFR15.2 | Recovery shall target RPO 24 hours and RTO four hours under a U2-owned recovery barrier. The barrier rejects new forecast admissions, pauses U7 consumers and outbox relays, waits until no broker delivery is unacknowledged and no publish confirm is pending, then records in PostgreSQL the highest committed inbox message ID, highest confirmed outbox message ID, queue/DLQ counts, and barrier ID. While workloads remain paused, it creates checksummed PostgreSQL snapshots and a RabbitMQ durable-volume/definitions snapshot and binds both to one signed recovery manifest. Only then may relays and consumers resume. Restore pointers unavailable, restore the bound pair, and reconcile every committed inbox effect, unpublished outbox row, queue/DLQ message ID, acknowledgement, attempt, and publication before ordinary processing resumes. | Every tagged portfolio release performs a clean-target drill plus crash fixtures before/after database commit, acknowledgement, publish confirm, barrier acquisition, each snapshot, manifest commit, and resume. Tests prove acknowledged inbound effects and confirmed outbound messages are either present or detected as a manifest discrepancy, duplicate replay is idempotent, snapshot age bounds observed loss to 24 hours, and total restore/reconciliation completes within four hours. | Abort the backup if quiescence or either snapshot fails and retain the previous valid manifest. On restore, keep pointers and messaging unavailable for any watermark/count/digest gap, require explicit Operator resolution, and never infer success, regenerate, or substitute forecasts silently. |

## Degraded behavior

Retail Data or Model Lifecycle unavailability blocks new run resolution but does
not rewrite the current pointer. Redis outage falls back to authorized governed
reads. Telemetry/OpenSearch outage cannot block authoritative commit but raises
observability-degraded state.
