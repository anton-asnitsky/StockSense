## Rules summary

| Group | Focus | Key outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BR1 | Tenant and role authority | Every read, mutation, job, and route is server-authorized. |
| BR2 | Source intake and lifecycle | Originals are immutable, bounded, versioned, and replay-safe. |
| BR3 | Extraction and validation | Partial evidence is explicit and remains non-authoritative. |
| BR4 | Accepted commercial terms | Managers create immutable non-overlapping term versions. |
| BR5 | Chunks and citations | Evidence is deterministic, traceable, and untrusted as instruction. |
| BR6 | Retrieval generations | Collections are isolated, reconciled, and atomically routed. |
| BR7 | Embedding evaluation | Both candidates receive comparable measured evaluation before selection. |
| BR8 | Supplier comparison | Eligible accepted terms are ordered transparently without opaque scoring. |
| BR9 | Integration and audit | Local transactions, outboxes, inboxes, and reconciliation replace distributed transactions. |
| BR10 | Recovery and portfolio evidence | Restores, local recovery fences, contracts, secrets, setup, and demonstrations fail honestly and reproducibly. |
| BR11 | Safe Redis cache | Five-minute result caching verifies authoritative source, term and route generations before hits and fills. |
