# Planning and Purchasing Reliability Requirements

Unit: U8 Planning and Purchasing (`planning-purchasing`)

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance and evidence | Failure behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NFR7.1 | A claimed Review Job shall use a 60-second lease renewed every 15 seconds; four missed renewals fence the worker. Each logical job allows three attempts with exponential backoff from 30 seconds to five minutes. Integrity, authority, incompatible-evidence, and deterministic calculation failures are terminal. | Interrupt claim, evidence resolution, calculation, heartbeat, result commit, and acknowledgement. Prove fencing, immutable prior attempts, no duplicate outcomes, same-job retry, unchanged allowance charge, and exact input-snapshot reuse. | Retry only eligible transient failures under the same job. Exhaustion records a durable Failed outcome; it never creates a new allowance charge or fabricates a recommendation. |
| NFR7.2 | RabbitMQ publication/consumption shall implement the C15 v2 construction baseline with transactional outbox/inbox, confirms, acknowledgement after inbox and business commit, five total delivery attempts including the initial delivery, one-to-30-second exponential backoff, seven-day DLQ retention, and audited replay batches of at most 100 with one active replay per queue. The legacy C15 v1 `maxRetries: unresolved-OQ-C15-01` value is non-authoritative. | Interrupt outbox relay, broker confirm, delivery, inbox insert, business commit, acknowledgement, every retry, DLQ routing, and replay. Prove C15 v2 schema and policy validation, message identity, tenant/placement revalidation, canonical digest, envelope limit, deduplication, no duplicate transition/receipt/stock effect, 30-second outbox alert, and 1,000-ready-message backlog alert. | Retry transport delivery without creating a business attempt. After the fifth total delivery failure, dead-letter visibly; replay the same message only under current authority and operation-idempotency rules. |
| NFR7.3 | Payload-bound idempotency, expected aggregate/stock versions, transaction locks, unique logical schedule keys, and owned module ports shall make policy, allowance, review, scenario, purchase, receipt, inventory, audit, and outbox effects atomic and replay-safe. | Crash-point and race tests cover every boundary before/after commit and response loss, including scheduled duplicates, fourth manual request, changed payload, stale version, concurrent decisions, cancellation/receipt, receipt/receipt, and multi-line all-or-nothing validation. | Return the original accepted result for exact replay, `409` for changed payload/state/version, or `422` for domain validation. Never expose or commit a partial business effect. |
| NFR9.1 | Holds and referenced dependencies override retention. Purchase proposals, orders, receipts, stock links, policies, preferences, accepted allowance charges, idempotency results, and dependent provenance remain for the life of the seeded dataset; review/scenario/recommendation history remains one year; failed/partial attempt detail and business audit 90 days; logs and dead letters seven days; recovery snapshots 30 days. | Time-travel, reference, expiry, audit/OpenSearch consistency, backup-expiry, restore, and rebuild tests prove dependencies prevent deletion and tombstones prevent expired data from reappearing. | Retain on uncertainty, expose cleanup failure, and never remove evidence needed to explain a live purchase or receipt. |
| NFR11.1 | A clean reviewer shall deploy the CPU-only local stack and execute policy, scheduled/manual review, deterministic recommendation, scenario, Draft, submit, approve/reject/cancel, partial/full receipt, retry, cache outage, broker failure, and restore flows without owner credentials, cached artifacts, cloud services, or GPU. | Evidence records pinned versions/checksums, commands, hardware, resources, requests, jobs/attempts, calculations, state versions, decisions, receipts/movements, alerts, recovery, and limitations. | Mark reproducibility failed; fixtures-only or owner-machine credentials cannot substitute for the real service path. |
| NFR13.2 | Flyway/routine, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI, numeric, transaction, state-machine, or module-port incompatibility shall block rollout. Rollback shall use immutable application versions and compatible schemas and preserve accepted purchases, receipts, and idempotency results. | Upgrade/rollback tests cover old/new contract overlap, migration order, routine grants, state transitions, in-flight jobs/messages, and receipt transaction compatibility. | Stop rollout and preserve the last compatible deployment; never downgrade by mutating or deleting accepted business history. |
| NFR15.1 | Over a 30-day seeded test, create exactly one logical scheduled job per retailer-local date and create or retain at least 99% by 08:05. When no older job blocks it and dependencies are healthy, claim by 08:10 and complete its 100-product calculation by 08:11. Retained work keeps its original local date, warns after ten minutes, alerts after 30 minutes, and starts within ten minutes of eligibility. | Exercise clock changes, duplicate schedulers, active manual jobs, restarts, broker delay, healthy dependencies, and failures across all seeded retailer-days. Publish exact job counts, admission/retention percentage, start/completion times, late reasons, and duplicate count. | Never redraft, duplicate, expire, or charge retained scheduled work. Alert with job/local-date/correlation evidence and preserve the one-active-job invariant. |
| NFR15.2 | Recovery shall target RPO 24 hours and RTO two hours for one seeded retailer. Reads and commands remain unavailable until placement, policies/preferences, logical review keys, allowance counts, evidence digests, latest/last-success pointers, transitions, approved/received/outstanding balances, expected arrivals, stock links, idempotency, audit, and outbox/inbox checkpoints reconcile. | Each tagged portfolio release performs a clean-target drill from a backup no older than 24 hours. Test crash points around Purchasing/Inventory commit, outbox publication, broker acknowledgement, snapshot, and restore; record elapsed time, loss, counts/digests, mismatches, repairs, and unavailable interval. | Keep affected resources unavailable, fence stale placement/workers, and use ordinary authorized retry or controlled operator recovery only; never repeat a receipt, reset allowance, invent a recommendation, or resurrect expired data. |

## Degraded behavior

An unavailable Forecasting, Supplier Knowledge, or Retail Data dependency blocks
new review admission or the affected product outcome according to the approved
workflow; it never triggers silent substitution. Redis outage uses bounded
governed fallback. Telemetry/OpenSearch outage cannot block authoritative
business commit but raises observability-degraded state.
